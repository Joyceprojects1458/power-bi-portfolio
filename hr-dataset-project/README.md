# HR Dataset Project — Power BI

My first Power BI project, built with a sample HR dataset.

No real employee information is included. This dataset is fictional, made up for portfolio practice.

## What this project answers

- How many people work here, and how fast are they leaving?
- Where is turnover happening, and why?
- How long does hiring take, and where do offers come from?
- How engaged are employees (eNPS), and what needs work?
- Is training getting done on time?

## The 5 pages

1. **HR Numbers** — the headline KPIs in one place
2. **Turnover** — terminations by year, department, type, and reason
3. **Hiring** — applicant funnel and Time to Fill
4. **Engagement** — eNPS trends and survey scores
5. **Training** — completion rates and overdue training

## What I found

- **Turnover is very high:** Out of 800 employees, there were 573 terminations — a 72% turnover rate. Most terminations were voluntary (78%), with only 22% involuntary. Terminations peaked in 2024 with 186. Branch Operations had the most terminations, about 210.
- **Hiring is going well:** Offer acceptance is 82%. Time to fill a position is around 39 days, close to the common average of about 40 days. Risk and Compliance hiring takes about 41 days, while HR hiring takes about 32 days. Only about 1% of applicants get an offer, so the selection process is very selective.
- **Engagement needs work:** There are more detractors than promoters. eNPS is -9.5, a mild detractor score, but the trend is improving. Survey scores are between 3.4 and 3.8. Employees feel Pay Fairness is low, while Manager Support is highest. Department scores differ a lot — IT scored high, while Contact Center and Collections scored low.
- **Training is mostly done, but overdue courses are a problem:** Mandatory training completion is 90.5%, but 450 courses are overdue. Branch Operations has the most overdue, at 155.

## Skills used

Power BI Desktop, DAX measures (KPIs, turnover rate, eNPS), slicers, funnel chart, trend lines, and multi-page report design.
